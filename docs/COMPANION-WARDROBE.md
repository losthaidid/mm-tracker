# Companion wardrobe, hobbies and background pictures

Open **Settings → Appearance & motion** or **Studio → Theme studio**.

- **Wardrobe:** 24 garment styles. Daily surprise chooses a stable combination for the local calendar day.
- **Outfit colours:** 12 palettes. With both fields set to Daily surprise, the 288 garment/colour combinations do not repeat within a cycle. A manually selected style or palette stays selected.
- **Momo’s accessory:** 33 accessories, plus None and Daily surprise. Party hat has its own artwork. Music headphones appear only when selected, or when selected by Daily surprise.
- **Let him enjoy quiet, automatic hobbies:** enables short activities between work. Tap the companion → **Wardrobe & hobbies** to try reading, painting, gardening, ukulele, tea, photography, astronomy, puzzles, origami, knitting, baking or chess immediately.

Use **Save theme** to keep wardrobe and hobby preferences with the account. Animation frames and daily outfit changes do not write to the cloud. Hobbies stay at his current position and stop for direct interaction, typing, editing, focus, privacy, presentation or a hidden page. Automatic hobbies respect reduced motion and Animations Off. A requested nap still requires clicking the companion to wake him.

The umbrella canopy, gripping sleeve and single hand now share the body's SVG coordinates and mirror together. The original arm on that side is hidden while he holds it.

## Your own background pictures

In Theme studio, choose **My background pictures → Add picture**. Use a JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 15 MB and 40 megapixels. The browser resizes it to at most 2560 × 1600, removes original file metadata by rendering a new JPEG, and keeps each stored picture below 1.2 MB.

Keep up to six pictures. Choose **Page background**, **My Day welcome**, or **Both**, adjust page picture strength, then **Save picture settings**. **Use theme background** switches back without deleting the gallery. **Remove** deletes an individual picture from this device.

Pictures are private to the account in this browser's IndexedDB storage. They are not sent to Supabase, exported with themes or included in JSON backups. Other devices need their own uploads; clearing browser site data removes the gallery. A failed storage write leaves the previous background active.

## TVs and large monitors

**Display rendering → Automatic** enables a bounded rendering profile on large viewports or high pixel counts. It removes large backdrop blur, full-page orbit/comet/sweep layers and 3D widget tilt while retaining the moving landscape and normal dashboard controls. Small resize changes use hysteresis to avoid repeatedly switching rendering modes.

Choose **Smooth · TVs and high-resolution monitors** to enable that profile on a TV using a smaller desktop resolution. **Full effects** keeps the regular desktop effects. Save the theme after choosing. The existing iPhone profile and reduced-motion preferences continue to apply.

The profile is tested with a 3840 × 2160 browser viewport; a physical TV's refresh rate, display cable and graphics driver need checking on that device if flicker continues.
