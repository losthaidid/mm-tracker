# Workspace experience

Implements Batch D 7, 10, 193, 196, 198 and 204, plus visual selections 241, 244, 247 and 249. Existing process and presentation features remain available. No database migration is needed.

## Where to start

- **Rule reminders → Maintain rules:** add, edit, pause or remove up to 40 personal rules. Conditions cover tickets without dated updates, overdue incomplete tasks, activities starting soon and current-year leave balances at or below a maintained threshold. Threshold units appear in the editor. Undated ticket history is not assigned an invented date. Closed and archived work is excluded.
- **Rule reminders:** review each matching record and reason, open its source, snooze it for one hour or dismiss the unchanged issue. A changed record or rule may appear again. Evaluation occurs while the website is open, after edits and at least once a minute; this is an inbox, not background notification delivery. Existing leave allowance booking checks remain in place.
- **Sync health:** inspect actual saving state, pending changes, cloud revision and availability of this browser's recovery copy. Cloud confirmation requires the existing save acknowledgement. Retry delegates to the existing sync control; recovery opens existing controls rather than replacing records automatically. The existing pending-save departure warning remains active.
- **Settings → Workspace → Light, companion & completion moments:** choose gradual local-time lighting or morning, afternoon, evening, night or the saved theme; maintain a reading/resting habitat; switch completion moments, expressions and greetings on or off. Manual lighting also controls the atmosphere window. Automatic lighting respects that window's existing local override.
- **Break Terrarium → Scenery → Moonlit observatory:** switch between the existing glass garden and an illustrated observatory. Scenery changes preserve the current countdown, pause and audio state. The moon dial uses the same timer; the repeating break-end alarm still stops only on explicit **Resume work**. Scenery is saved on this device for the current account.
- **Tickets:** folder tabs show status; identifiers, latest update, ownership, priority, deadlines, filtering and record actions retain their existing behavior. Closed folders have quieter colours.
- **Settings → Companion & sound → Soundscape:** artwork follows the dominant channel in your mix and shows actual playing, paused or muted state. Rest music has separate artwork. There is no microphone or beat-analysis processing.

## Companion and effects

The illustrated home follows sleeping, reading or resting activity, with an optional reading/resting preference. Moving the existing home continues to use its existing location controls. After **Go home and nap**, only clicking the companion wakes him; arrival greetings and completion reactions never wake him.

Brief completion moments rotate between blooms, lanterns and stars. Companion reactions cover completed work and returning from breaks. Occasional arrival/return greetings have a 30-minute cooldown and wait around typing, dialogs, focus, privacy and presentation. The companion's existing quiet/greeting settings also take precedence.

New illustrations use local SVG/CSS without image downloads or new dependencies. Celebrations contain at most nine symbols (five on narrow screens), expire after about 2.4 seconds and replace any previous burst. Hidden-page and reduced-motion safeguards stop decorative activity; phone layouts reduce observatory detail. Aurora remains removed.

## Saving and verification

Rules, dismissals and experience preferences use the existing account document, backup and restore flow. Stale account forms cannot save into another account. Sound mixes, scenery and greeting cooldowns remain local preferences; no background cloud polling was added.

Automated checks cover rule validation/matching, acknowledgement behavior, honest sync states, settings persistence, account changes, bounded effects and scenery changes without resetting timers or alarms. Browser QA uses fictional records on desktop and at 390px width. This does not substitute for a physical iPhone or screen-sharing test.
