## Ticket matching

Matching uses deterministic text rules on the signed-in account's current tickets, without AI, network requests or record changes. Searches are briefly debounced and the bounded index is rebuilt after records change. Exact descriptions and ticket identifiers rank highly; shared meaningful words/codes also match. Results explain the shared terms, rather than presenting a confidence percentage. They are suggestions for the user to assess, not proof of duplicate issues. Editing excludes the current ticket. History and user text stay escaped; the existing ticket save, status, milestone and history flows remain in charge.

## Screenshot workflow

The editor fills the browser window. Choose a tool and drag on the screenshot. **Move** permits scrolling/panning, including touch scrolling on phones. Brush size and colour apply to marks; arrow strokes use a finer width. **Crop** chooses the exported area. **Compare original** is only an editing view: exports always contain the current edits and crop. Solid covers replace pixels in the exported image; blur softens detail. Originals remain available in the draft. **Add edited image to a guide** transfers a flattened image with its title/caption.

**Numbers** adds numbered screenshot markers: click to place 1, 2, 3 and drag before releasing to adjust the placement. Numbering restarts for each screenshot and resumes after the highest saved marker. Use **Colour** and **Marker size** to style new markers. Labels automatically use a readable contrast colour, and bubbles stay within the image edges. **Rectangle outline** lets you drag an unfilled border around any area; **Colour** and **Border thickness** style new outlines. Existing arrows, covers, blur and crop remain available. Both new tools support Undo/Redo, Compare original, Save/Open editable guide, live preview and PDF/JPG/PNG export in all three guide layouts. They also work in Screenshot privacy.

**Export layout** offers **Guided walkthrough** (numbered, full-width screenshots with short steps sharing pages), **Split view** (screenshots on the left and instructions on the right), and **Quick reference card** (two-column step cards, with unused page height trimmed). New guides start with Guided walkthrough. Older editable guides keep their original one-step-per-page layout until another layout is chosen. The selected layout is saved in editable guides and used by every PDF/JPG/PNG guide export. Quick-reference PDFs use the same compact page proportions as their exported images; the other layouts use A4.

Changing the layout opens **Live export preview**, selects **Full page** and updates automatically. The full-page and footer views support both A4 and compact page dimensions. Colours, footer transparency, logo size/position, crops and annotations carry across layouts. Long instructions continue without losing text, and footer space remains reserved on every page.

**Export heading** controls the small banner at the top of every guide page, separately from the guide title. Replace `WORKHUB / SCREENSHOT GUIDE` with your organisation or guide label (up to 180 characters), or leave it blank to hide the banner. Long headings wrap above the title and are repeated on continuation pages. Editing it clears an already-prepared download; prepare the guide again to include the change.

**Live preview, export colours, footer logo & watermark** opens the guide branding controls. Choose Green, Blue, Purple or Charcoal, or maintain custom **Accent colour** and **Page background** values. These apply to PDF/JPG/PNG guide pages, independently of the website theme. Text adjusts for readability against custom backgrounds; screenshots and uploaded logos retain their colours.

Add a transparent PNG/WebP logo (up to 2 MB), optional footer text (120 characters), Left/Center/Right alignment and Small/Medium/Large logo size. **Footer opacity** (5-100%) fades the logo and footer text for a watermark effect. The editor shows transparency on a checkerboard; the exported footer has no background box. Existing opaque image backgrounds are preserved. JPG cannot carry alpha transparency, so a transparent logo is composited directly against the selected page colour.

Fine logo controls add **Logo width**, **Horizontal position** and **Vertical position** sliders. Wide footer images can fill the page content width; aspect ratio is preserved within the footer area. The printed A4 dimensions are shown beside the controls. Alignment and size presets remain available; **Reset logo placement** restores the original centered medium placement. Exact adjustments are retained in editable guides, and older guides retain their original placement.

**Live export preview** updates automatically while the branding panel is open. Switch between **Full page** and **Footer close-up** to inspect the first exported page, including its colours, heading, screenshot and footer. The preview shares the export renderer; full downloads still include every step and caption continuation. Rendering is debounced and limited to one page/job at a time. Closing the panel/editor or switching accounts cancels pending work and releases the preview canvas.

Branding repeats on every guide page, including caption continuations. Space is reserved above the unchanged step/page numbering, keeping long captions clear of the footer. Save/Open editable guides retain colours, footer settings and the embedded logo. Older guides default to the original green palette and no footer. Logo imports stay local, preserve alpha and are normalized to a maximum of 1600 × 600 pixels; replacing a logo or changing branding invalidates prepared downloads.

PNG exports contain the edited image. Guide PDF/JPG/PNG pages also contain the maintained heading, step numbers, titles and captions. JPG and PNG page collections download as ZIP files with numbered pages; JPG uses actual JPEG encoding. Long captions continue onto extra numbered pages. PDF text is rasterized for Unicode font compatibility and is not selectable. Prepare the file, then select its explicit download link. PDF ZIP is available for browsers that intercept a direct PDF download.

Images and guide drafts stay in this tab while signed in; they do not sync to Supabase or appear in account backups. Closing the editor keeps the current local draft. **Save editable guide** downloads a `.workhub-guide.json` file; **Open editable guide** restores its heading, steps and edits atomically after validation. Older editable files open with the original WorkHub heading; an explicitly blank heading stays blank. Editable files contain original images as well as annotations: use the exported PDF/JPG/PNG for sharing. Replacing a local draft never changes cloud records. Account changes and leaving the page clear local images, drafts and ready links; pending file operations are cancelled.

## Limits and verification

Inputs: PNG, JPEG or WebP, at most 8 MB and 12 megapixels each, normalized to a maximum edge of 1,800 pixels. Guides: up to 24 steps, 12 million combined normalized pixels, 18 million image-data characters, 200 annotations per screenshot and 120 export pages. Editable imports are limited to 24 MB and accept only embedded raster images, bounded coordinates and plain text. Unsupported/corrupt inputs are rejected before replacing the current draft. Image dimensions are checked before decoding. No new dependency, database migration or authentication change is added.

`batch-c-engine.js` owns matching and document limits; `batch-c-files.js` owns local decoding, flattening and exports; `batch-c.js` owns Studio/editor integration and account cleanup; `batch-c.css` owns the responsive layout.
