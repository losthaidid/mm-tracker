Your Google Drive files and privacy

WorkHub is a personal work organizer. Its optional Google Drive connection stores files you choose to upload, Process Hub exports and Handover pack snapshots in your own Google account.

What Google access is used

The connection requests the drive.file permission. WorkHub creates a dedicated “My WorkHub files” folder, uploads original files without converting their format, reads file metadata and manages access for individual files created by this application. It does not request access to your entire Drive.

Google provides your account’s display name, email and Drive account identifier so the app can display the connected account and avoid changing a file from another account.

Where information is stored

File contents are stored in Google Drive. File names, sizes, dates, Drive links, linked work identifiers and the public Google client ID are stored in your account’s private WorkHub workspace, hosted in Supabase. That workspace may also be cached in your browser and included in backups you download.

Google access tokens stay only in this tab’s memory. They are not saved in the workspace, browser storage, backups or QR codes. Google authorization code or client secrets are not collected.

Sharing and QR codes

A personal QR contains the file’s Drive download or viewing link and uses its existing Google permissions. WorkHub generates QR images locally. It does not send those links to a separate QR service.

Creating a sharing QR requires a separate review for that file. This can grant anyone who receives its link permission to read and download it. Disabling sharing removes only the public reader permission recorded as added here, leaving other permissions and already downloaded copies unchanged. Your Google Workspace policies may restrict sharing.

Your controls

Drive access is optional. You can continue using local exports without connecting. Use Settings → Google Drive setup → Disconnect to clear this device’s connection and revoke the app’s Google grant, or remove access in Google account connections. Files remain in Drive until you delete them there. You can review, download, delete files and change their permissions directly in Google Drive.

WorkHub does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models. It uses Google information only for the file storage, download and sharing actions you request. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This self-hosted site’s operator controls its Google Cloud project and support contact. Use the support email shown on the Google consent screen for questions about that deployment.
